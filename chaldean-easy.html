Chaldean Easy

Learn spoken Chaldean (Soorath) the fun way

Download on the App Store

Chaldean Easy is the first app designed to teach spoken Chaldean (Soorath / Syriac / Assyrian) to English speakers, with real human voices, cartoon characters and bite-sized daily lessons.

What's inside

  • 24 units covering greetings, family, food, body parts, verbs and more
  • Real recordings by native Chaldean speakers (male and female voices)
  • Games: Bubble Pop, Word Scramble, Matching Pairs, True or False and more
  • Flashcards, dialogues and cultural proverbs
  • Daily learning path with XP and streaks
  • Kids Mode, a grammar reference guide and a Chaldean alphabet explorer

Who it's for

Iraqi Christian diaspora families, people reconnecting with their heritage, and anyone curious about one of the world's oldest living languages.

Price

Unit 1 is free, no account needed. Full access is $14.99/month or $99.99/year.
