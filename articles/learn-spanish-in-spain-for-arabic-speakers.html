Learn Spanish in Spain, in Arabic: A Guide for Newcomers

The Spanish you need first

Newcomers need greetings, numbers, restaurants, transport and the doctor, plus the vocabulary of paperwork: empadronamiento, NIE and TIE, opening a bank account, phone and internet.

Two grammar traps

Ser vs estar and tú vs usted confuse many learners. Learn them with short examples, in your own language.

Learn with Arabic support

Isbani Sahl – Learn Spanish (إسباني سهل) has 60 lessons in 11 units, entirely in Arabic, with a picture, Spanish audio, Arabic audio and an Arabic-letter pronunciation hint for every word. Unit 1 is free.
