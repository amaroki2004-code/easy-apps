25 Everyday Iraqi Arabic Phrases (with Pronunciation)

Iraqi Arabic sounds very different from the Modern Standard Arabic taught in most courses. If you want to talk with Iraqi family and friends, start with the phrases people actually use every day. Pronunciation below follows the Baghdadi dialect; accents vary by city and family.

Greetings and small talk

Iraqi ArabicSay itMeaning
شلونك؟shlonak? (to a man) / shlonich? (to a woman)How are you?
زين، الحمد للهzein, il-hamdu lillahGood, thank God
شكو ماكو؟shaku maku?What's new? What's going on?
ماكو شيmaku shiNothing much
هلا واللهhala wallahHey, welcome! (warm greeting)
الله وياكallah wiyakGoodbye (God be with you)
تسلمtislamThanks (bless you)
عيونيeyooniMy dear (literally "my eyes")

At the table

Iraqi ArabicSay itMeaning
تمنtimmanRice
خبزkhubuzBread
مايmaiWater
چايchaiTea
بالعافيةbil-afyaEnjoy your meal / to your health
شبعتshbaatI'm full
طيب هوايةtayyib hwayaVery tasty
كافيkafiEnough, that's enough

Iraqi Arabic uses the letter چ (ch) and گ (g), which don't exist in standard Arabic: chai (tea) and bachir (tomorrow) use the ch sound.
