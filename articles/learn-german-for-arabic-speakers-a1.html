Learn German for Arabic Speakers: Your A1 Roadmap

What A1 means

A1 is the first level of the European language framework. It covers greetings, numbers, shopping, appointments, simple forms and talking about the past in basic terms. Many people need A1 for a family visa.

Learn in your own language

Explanations in Arabic remove a big barrier. Pronunciation written in Arabic letters lets you read German aloud from day one, and Arabic translations of answers explain why each answer is right.

Practice the four exam parts

A1 exams test listening, reading, writing and speaking. Practice each part with short exercises and keep key phrases such as introductions, numbers and times ready.

The app

German Easy: Arabic Edition has 76 lessons in 14 units covering A1, A1 exam practice with Arabic explanations (its own practice material, not official exam questions), funny everyday dialogues and a journey map across Germany. Unit 1 is free and the monthly plan has a 3-day free trial.
