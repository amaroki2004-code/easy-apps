Isbani Sahl (إسباني سهل) teaches the Spanish spoken in Spain to Arabic speakers. The whole app, including menus, explanations and word meanings, is in Arabic.

What's inside

  • 60 lessons in 11 units, from greetings and numbers to restaurants, shopping, the doctor and work
  • A "Living in Spain" unit: government offices, empadronamiento, NIE and TIE, opening a bank account
  • Clear help with ser vs estar, and tú vs usted
  • Every word has a picture, Spanish audio, Arabic audio and a pronunciation hint in Arabic letters
  • Dialogues, quizzes, listening games and a Word Bank
  • No account, no ads, no tracking

Who it's for

Arabs living in or moving to Spain, travelers and students.

Price

Unit 1 is free. Pro unlocks all units and games (monthly or yearly, with a free trial for new subscribers).
