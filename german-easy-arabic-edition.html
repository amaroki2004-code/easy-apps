German Easy is the German course built for Arabic speakers, from zero to level A1. Every explanation and menu is in Arabic, every German word and sentence has clear German audio, and pronunciation is written in Arabic letters so you can read German from day one.

What's inside

  • 76 lessons in 14 units covering level A1
  • A1 exam practice: listening, reading, writing and speaking, with Arabic explanations (our own practice material, not official exam questions)
  • Pronunciation written in Arabic script
  • Short, funny dialogues from everyday life in Germany
  • Journey map across Germany, unlimited flashcards and quizzes
  • Culture tips such as when to say du and Sie

Who it's for

Anyone preparing for an A1 German exam (for example for a spouse visa), newcomers to Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and students and workers who want a strong start.

Price

Unit 1 is free. German Easy Pro unlocks everything, with a 3-day free trial on the monthly plan and a 1-week free trial on the yearly plan.
