Watermelon Scanner

Pick a ripe watermelon with your iPhone camera

Download on the App Store

Watermelon Scanner uses your iPhone camera and AI to instantly score watermelon ripeness: no guessing, no tapping, no wasted melons. Point, tap, know.

What's inside

  • Free scan: photograph a watermelon and get a Ripeness Score (0-100) based on color, field spot and skin texture, with expert tips
  • Pro scan ($2.99 one-time): guided 3-step analysis of overhead color, field spot photo and stem condition, with a detailed breakdown by category
  • Scan history: every scan is saved with its photo and score so you can compare melons
  • No account required, no data collected, works entirely on your device

Who it's for

Farmers markets, grocery shopping, backyard BBQs, and anyone who wants a perfectly ripe watermelon every time.

Price

Free scan included. Pro scan is a $2.99 one-time purchase.
