Practical American English for Arabic speakers

أمريكا سهلة — الإنجليزية العملية للحياة في أمريكا، بالعربي

America Easy (أمريكا سهلة) teaches Arabic speakers the American English they need in real life: the bank, the DMV, the doctor, school, renting an apartment and job interviews. The whole app, including menus, explanations and word meanings, is in Arabic.

What's inside

  • 57 lessons in 8 levels, on a road trip across America from New York to Los Angeles
  • Real-life situations: renting an apartment, the bank, the DMV and your driver's license, jobs and job interviews, your kids' school, the doctor and pharmacy, government offices, money and bills
  • Spelling your name, email and address in English: "How do you spell that?"
  • Every word has a picture, clear American audio, the Arabic meaning and a pronunciation hint written in Arabic letters
  • Tips for the sounds Arabic speakers find hard: P, V, TH and CH
  • Short dialogues, quizzes, listening games, a Word Bank, Kids Mode and Sahl, your travel buddy
  • No account, no ads, no tracking

Who it's for

Arabs living in the US, and people getting ready to move there for work, study or to join family.

Price

Level 1 is free, plus two preview lessons from later levels. Pro unlocks all levels and games for $7.99/month or $59.99/year, with a free trial for new subscribers.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app to learn practical English for life in America, in Arabic?

Yes. America Easy (أمريكا سهلة) teaches Arabic speakers the English they need for daily life in the US: the bank, the DMV, the doctor and pharmacy, the kids' school, renting an apartment and job interviews. All menus and explanations are in Arabic.

Do I need to know any English to start?

No. Every word comes with a picture, American audio, the Arabic meaning and a pronunciation hint written in Arabic letters, so complete beginners can start from Level 1.

Does America Easy help with hard English sounds like P, V and TH?

Yes. It includes tips for the sounds Arabic speakers often find difficult, such as P, V, TH and CH.

How much does America Easy cost?

Level 1 and two preview lessons are free. Pro unlocks all 57 lessons for $7.99 per month or $59.99 per year, with a free trial for new subscribers.
