Learn Iraqi Arabic Easy

Iraqi dialect, not textbook Arabic

Download on the App Store

Most Arabic apps teach Modern Standard Arabic, the formal language of news and textbooks. Learn Iraqi Arabic Easy teaches the everyday Iraqi dialect spoken by family and friends, recorded by real native Iraqi speakers.

What's inside

  • 800+ real Iraqi Arabic words and phrases in 65+ structured lessons
  • Greetings, family, food, shopping, travel, jobs, emotions, emergencies and more
  • Animated two-speaker conversations
  • Simple grammar: verb conjugation, past tense, pronouns
  • Flashcards, matching and fill-in-the-blank games
  • Cultural tips behind the words

Who it's for

Kids and grandkids of Iraqi immigrants, partners marrying into an Iraqi family, travelers, and anyone who got stuck on textbook Arabic.

Free to download.
